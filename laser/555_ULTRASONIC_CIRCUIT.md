# 555 Timer Ultrasonic Alarm Calculation

## Circuit Parameters
- **Resistor $R_1$**: $20\text{ k}\Omega$
- **Potentiometer $R_2$**: $2\text{ k}\Omega$
- **Timing Capacitor $C_1$**: $2\text{ nF}$ ($0.002\text{ }\mu\text{F}$)

## Frequency Formula
$$f = \frac{1.44}{(R_1 + 2R_2) \cdot C_1}$$

## Calculation
$$f = \frac{1.44}{(20,000 + 4,000) \times (2 \times 10^{-9})} = \frac{1.44}{4.8 \times 10^{-5}} = 30,000\text{ Hz} = 30\text{ kHz}$$

This generates a **$30\text{ kHz}$ high-frequency ultrasonic deterrent sound** that frightens animals away without causing injury.
