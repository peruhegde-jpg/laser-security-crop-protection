# Tinkercad Simulation Instructions

1. Open [Tinkercad Circuits](https://www.tinkercad.com) and click **+ Create -> Circuit**.
2. Add:
   - 1x Arduino Uno R3
   - 1x Breadboard
   - 1x Photoresistor (LDR)
   - 1x Piezo Speaker
   - 1x Red LED & 1x Green LED
   - 1x 10kΩ Resistor & 2x 220Ω Resistors
3. Connect components as shown in `src/circuit_schematic.txt`.
4. Click **Code** -> Change dropdown to **Text**.
5. Paste code from `src/laser_security.ino`.
6. Click **Start Simulation**.
7. Click the **LDR** and drag the light slider to test intrusion detection!
