=====================================================
            LASER SECURITY CIRCUIT WIRING
=====================================================

1. LDR SENSING CIRCUIT:
   +5V ---> [LDR Photocell] ---> Node A0 ---> [10kΩ Resistor] ---> GND

2. ARDUINO PIN CONNECTIONS:
   - Analog Pin A0  : Connected between LDR and 10kΩ resistor
   - Digital Pin 7  : NE555 Alarm Trigger / Reset Pin
   - Digital Pin 8  : Piezo Speaker (+) terminal
   - Digital Pin 9  : Red LED (+) via 220Ω resistor to GND
   - Digital Pin 10 : Green LED (+) via 220Ω resistor to GND
   - GND Pin        : Common Ground Rail
   - 5V Pin         : +5V Power Rail
